# Content style guide

- Short, plain sentences. Use the student's own numbers. No "ancient secrets".
- Every historical statement cites a source in `claims`; dates carry their uncertainty.
- Say "attributed to" when a result is known only through later texts (Mādhava).
- Comparisons with other traditions need a scholarly source and neutral wording.
- Sanskrit terms in IAST with diacritics in English text; the app shows them in the reader's script on verse cards.
- Translations keep the translator's wording and spelling; explain old words with glossary notes.
- Every `ltext` has `en` and `kn`. Machine translation is a first draft only.
